Remote Clock – WFH Friday Instructions

Complete step-by-step instructions for installing, logging in, clocking in/out, managing account settings, and securely using the Remote Clock application.

1

Install the App

  1. Open Google Play Store.
  2. Search for Remote Clock.
  3. Tap Install.

    4. For Android APK installer: click here!

    For iOS device: click here!

2

Open Remote Clock

Locate the Remote Clock application from your home screen or app drawer and open it.

3

Log In

Enter your credentials:

  • Company: bangsamoro

  • Password: Changeme123!

Tap Login.

If you forgot your password, tap Forgot Password?
4

Main Dashboard

  • Your Name
  • Current Date & Time
  • Schedule Information
  • IN Button
  • OUT Button
  • Announcements
  • Today's Timelogs
5

Enable Fingerprint Authentication

  1. Tap Enable.
  2. Accept Terms & Conditions.
  3. Register your fingerprint.
6

Account Information & Settings

Available information:

  • Name
  • Employee Number
  • Access Number
  • Company
  • Position
  • Email Address
  • SSS Number
  • PhilHealth Number
  • Pag-IBIG Number
  • TIN Number
7

Logout

  1. Open Account Information.
  2. Tap Logout.
